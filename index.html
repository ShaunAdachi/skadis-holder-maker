How to shoot
1. Print the measuring sheet (A4 or US Letter) at 100% (no "fit to page"). Check that the 150 mm marks measure 150 mm.
2. Lay the tool flat in the middle of the sheet, along its long side, tip pointing up.
3. Shoot straight down from about 60 cm with 2× zoom, lit from above with no hard shadows.
4. Use the original photo file. Then pick Slot, Round hole or Tray and export the STL.
